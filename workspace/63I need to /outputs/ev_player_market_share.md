**Key Observations:**
- Tata Motors has been the segment leader but lost some ground to MG, Mahindra, and BYD in Q1 FY25.
- MG's share has grown significantly due to models like the ZS EV and Comet EV.
- Mahindra's share recovered with the launch of the XUV400.
- Hyundai's share has declined from earlier years despite the introduction of the Kona Electric.
