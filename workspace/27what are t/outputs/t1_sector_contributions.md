# Sector Contributions to India's Economic Growth

Based on the CRISIL Passenger Vehicle Industry Report, the following details the contribution of different sectors to India's economic growth, including specific statistics, growth rates, and qualitative insights.

## Sectoral Contribution to GVA (FY24)

The report provides the breakdown of Gross Value Added (GVA) by sector for Fiscal Year 2024:

- **Services:** 54.6%
- **Industry:** 30.9%
- **Agriculture:** 14.5%

**Long-term Trends (FY15–FY24):** The services sector's share in GVA increased from 52.4% to 54.6%, while agriculture's share declined from 16.5% to 14.5%. The industrial sector's share fluctuated between approximately 29.6% and 31.6% over this period.

## Qualitative Insights by Sector

### Services Sector

**Role:** The services sector is the largest contributor to India's GDP, accounting for 54.6% of GVA in FY24. It has been a primary leader of economic growth, though its share dipped during the pandemic due to impacts on contact-sensitive industries.

**Key Drivers:**
- **Trade, Hotels, Transport, and Communication (THTC):** This sub-sector saw strong growth (12% in FY23) driven by pent-up post-pandemic demand, though growth has moderated recently (5.1% in Q4 FY24) as that demand fades.
- **Financial, Real Estate, and Professional Services:** Growth here (7.6% in Q4 FY24) is driven by a healthy banking sector, robust real estate activity, and strong credit momentum.
- **Public Administration & Defence:** This segment also contributed to growth (7.8% in Q4 FY24).

### Manufacturing/Industry Sector

**Role:** The industrial sector is the second-largest contributor, maintaining a ~30-31% share of GVA. It logged a 7.0% CAGR between FY15 and FY19, supported by government initiatives.

**Key Drivers:**
- **Government Initiatives:** The "Make in India" initiative, rising domestic consumption, and GST implementation improved the Ease of Doing Business ranking, supporting industrial output.
- **Construction & Utilities:** In FY23, the industrial sector's growth was strongly driven by utility services and construction (9.4% growth), which outpaced other industrial segments. Construction continues to be supported by government capital expenditure (capex) in infrastructure.
- **Manufacturing:** While manufacturing growth moderated in recent quarters (7% in Q1 FY25), capacity utilization is at a decadal high, creating conducive conditions for private investment.

### Agriculture Sector

**Role:** Agriculture acts as a stabilizer during economic downturns. It was the only sector to grow (4.0%) in FY21 when services and industry contracted, thereby restricting the fall in overall GDP. Its share in GVA was 14.5% in FY24.

**Key Drivers:**
- **Monsoon & Crop Output:** Growth is heavily influenced by monsoon performance and crop yields. For instance, Q1 FY25 saw a pickup to 2.0% growth due to a healthy *rabi* harvest (wheat production up 3.1%). Conversely, heat waves in April-May adversely impacted production.
- **Rural Demand:** A normal monsoon and increased agricultural production are expected to ease food inflation, which in turn supports rural demand and discretionary spending.
